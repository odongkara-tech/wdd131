Before setting out

  • Check the temple’s official page for current schedules, closures, and appointment information.
  • Confirm the route, transportation, parking, and expected travel time.
  • Allow room in your schedule for rest, meals, and the needs of everyone travelling with you.
  • Use the temple’s official contact details for questions about accessibility or special circumstances.
