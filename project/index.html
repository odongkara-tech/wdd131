    The details matter.
    So does the feeling.

    A thoughtful visit is easier when the practical details are settled. Start with these simple steps, then check the official temple information before you travel.

    Open the visit planner ↗
    1. 01

      Check before you go

      Confirm current schedules, appointment details, and visitor access with the official temple listing.

    2. 02

      Plan the practicals

      Allow time for the journey, parking, meals, and any needs your travel companions may have.

    3. 03

      Leave space to reflect

      Keep your plan flexible. A calm visit can be more rewarding than a packed itinerary.
