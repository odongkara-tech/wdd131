Temple Trail Guide

Website Planning Document

1. Site Overview

Temple Trail Guide is a faith-centered travel and discovery website created to help members of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and interested visitors learn about temples across the world. The site will make it easy for users to explore temple locations, understand their purposes, prepare for visits, and plan meaningful trips.

The overall goal is to combine inspiring content with practical planning tools so the experience feels both welcoming and useful. The website will be designed to help users discover temples, review travel essentials, and connect with a community of people who value meaningful family-focused experiences.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Temple Trail Guide is to serve as a welcoming and informative hub for individuals seeking guidance on temple travel, discovery, and preparation. The site will provide users with practical information about temple locations, meaningful travel planning, and inspirational resources that support a deeper appreciation for these sacred destinations.

In scope, the website will offer a clear overview of temple experiences, destination highlights, planning tips, and helpful content for families, couples, and visitors who are preparing for a temple visit. It will also provide a user-friendly structure for exploring relevant information and accessing contact resources through a simple and accessible experience.

3. Target Audience

Primary Audience

  • Members of the Church seeking temple information and planning resources.
  • Families planning meaningful vacations or sacred visits.
  • Young adults and couples preparing for temple trips.

Secondary Audience

  • Visitors who want to learn about temple architecture and history.
  • Travel planners looking for destination inspiration.
  • Friends and relatives supporting loved ones on temple-related trips.

4. Mission Statement

Temple Trail Guide exists to help individuals and families discover meaningful temple experiences through clear information, thoughtful planning, and a welcoming digital experience. The mission of the site is to inspire confidence, support informed travel decisions, and create a respectful online space where users can learn about temples and prepare for memorable visits.

5. Scenarios

  • What are the most meaningful temple destinations for a family trip, and how can we plan a visit that fits our schedule?
  • Where can I find helpful information about temple etiquette, travel preparation, and the best times to visit?
  • How can I learn more about a specific temple’s history, architecture, or location before planning a visit?

6. Brand Personality

The tone of the site will be warm, respectful, calm, and uplifting. It should feel trustworthy and inviting while still being polished and modern. The visual language should reflect peace, faith, and thoughtful travel without feeling overly crowded or decorative.

7. Site Name, Logo, and Domain

Site Name

Temple Trail Guide has been selected as the primary brand name for this project because it clearly communicates the central purpose of the website: helping users discover, plan, and appreciate temple journeys. The word "trail" conveys movement and discovery, while "guide" reflects dependable, helpful direction. Together, these terms establish a name that is memorable, relevant, and aligned with the intended user experience.

  • Domain Idea: templetrailguide.com
  • Tagline: Discover meaningful temple experiences and plan your next journey.

Logo Concept

The proposed logo combines a simplified temple silhouette with a winding trail motif to represent both sacred destinations and the journey required to reach them. This visual approach supports the brand’s tone of reverence, clarity, and hospitality while aligning with the website’s overall green, gold, and earth-toned palette.

8. Color Schema

The site’s color scheme is designed to feel peaceful, welcoming, and reverent while remaining modern and easy to read. The palette combines deep green, warm gold, soft sand, and natural brown tones to reflect sacred spaces, travel, and thoughtful planning.

  • Forest Green (#173C35): used for primary buttons and key details to establish a calm, grounded identity.
  • Warm Gold (#C6A66C): used as an accent for highlights and decorative details.
  • Soft Sand (#F7F5EF): used for page backgrounds and neutral space.
  • Muted Moss (#647D69): used for secondary labels and supporting emphasis.

9. Typography

Heading Font: Playfair Display, Georgia, Times New Roman, serif
Body Font: DM Sans, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif

This combination creates a timeless, respectful, and highly readable visual identity. The serif headings reinforce a sense of tradition and reverence, while the sans-serif body text supports modern clarity and usability across desktop and mobile screens.

  • Playfair Display, Georgia, Times New Roman, serif: used for main headings, section titles, and prominent text where a refined, formal tone is desired.
  • DM Sans, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif: used for paragraphs, lists, navigation text, and general content to ensure legibility and accessibility.

12. Content Strategy

  • Use concise, helpful text that is easy to scan on mobile and desktop screens.
  • Provide a searchable worldwide temple directory using official names, locations, statuses, and profile photos; label representative photos for dedicated temples and leave announced temples without a photo.
  • Present practical planning information such as timing, travel preparation, and family-friendly considerations.
  • Keep the site accessible by using descriptive headings, alt text, and strong contrast.

13. Accessibility and Usability Goals

  • Use sufficient color contrast for all text and interactive elements.
  • Ensure keyboard-friendly navigation for links and form controls.
  • Include clear headings and predictable layout patterns.
  • Design for mobile responsiveness, with content stacking cleanly on smaller screens.
  • Use optimized images and lazy loading to improve performance.

14. Implementation Notes

The project includes a responsive homepage, an Explore page with all entries listed in the official worldwide temple directory as of October 9, 2026, a Plan a Visit page with a browser-saved planning form, and a separate references page linked from each footer. Search and status filters make the global directory easier to browse. The directory has 385 entries. Dedicated temples without their own profile photo use a clearly labeled representative photo of the Accra Ghana Temple, while announced temples are displayed without a photo. Other profiles without an image identify the photo as unavailable. Directory photos are lazy-loaded as visitors browse 24 cards at a time. Destination cards and visit plans use localStorage. Official temple profile links provide current schedules and visitor information; saved visit reminders do not make bookings or transmit personal data. The homepage's temple photographs are optimized and stored locally; external directory imagery and references are credited on the references page.
